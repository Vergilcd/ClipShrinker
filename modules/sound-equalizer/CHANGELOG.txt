SOUND EQUALIZER - CHANGELOG
===========================

1.0.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.6.0)
- New: five bands (60 Hz, 250 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz, 12 kHz; -12 to +12 dB) and Volume (-20 to +20 dB) on a card
  over the preview, opened with the equalizer button next to full screen (also in full screen). Drag a slider
  or type a value; heard in the preview, used in the export (every audio track), saved with projects. A
  limiter catches peaks when something is boosted.
