TIMELINE EFFECTS - CHANGELOG
============================

1.3.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.9.1)
- The Effects window is now "Effect settings" (the gear button under the preview, or F2). Each block is headed
  by the same coloured button as under the preview, with its key, and Pause has its own block.
- The sound choices say what you hear: "Tape-wind at start and end" / "Just the sped-up sound", "Deep thump and
  swell" / "Just the slowed sound". "VHS look on the picture"; "Section volume" and "Whoosh volume".
- The play buttons are gone: hold a volume slider to listen. A sample loops at that volume while you drag it
  and stops when you let go.

1.2.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.9.0)
- New: Pause (an orange line, P): the picture stops on that frame for the length typed in its box, then the
  clip goes on from there. The clip's own sound is silent meanwhile; Sound track sounds keep playing.

1.1.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.5.0)
- Clip transitions moved out of this plugin: they are an app feature now, with their own Transition button
  next to "Clear all clips", and work with the plugin switched off.

1.0.0
- Split out of Clip Shrinker 2.3 into its own plugin: speed up / slow motion / zoom sections, the effect
  buttons under the preview, the Effects window (F2), clip transitions and RIFE slow-motion smoothing.
- RIFE now lives in this plugin's folder (plugins\timeline-effects\rife). A RIFE folder in library\ from
  older versions is moved here on the first start.
