PICTURE ADJUSTMENTS - CHANGELOG
===============================

1.1.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.5.0)
- New: Denoise (0 to 100 %) on the Picture card, for grain and noise in dark or low-light footage. Runs on the
  graphics card through OpenCL, or on the CPU (slower exports) when OpenCL is not available. Not used on clips
  that stay HDR.

1.0.0
- New: brightness, contrast and saturation (-100 to +100 %) for every clip, on a card over the preview (sun
  button next to full screen, also in full screen). Drag a slider or type a value; used in the preview and the
  export, saved with projects.
