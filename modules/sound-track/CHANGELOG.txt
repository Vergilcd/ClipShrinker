SOUND TRACK - CHANGELOG
=======================

1.0.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.7.0)
- New: a Sounds lane under each clip's timeline. Drop sound files (mp3, wav, ogg, m4a, aac, flac, opus) on it,
  or drag them from the Sounds list (each has a + to add it at the playhead; A adds the highlighted one).
  Drag a sound to move it, drag its ends to trim it, right-click or Delete removes it. Each sound has its own
  volume (-20 to +20 dB) and fade in / out. Sounds play in the preview, are mixed into the clip's first audio
  track in the export, and are saved with projects. Your sounds live in modules\sound-track\sounds (created
  at start-up).
