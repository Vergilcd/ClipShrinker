BITRATE PRESETS - CHANGELOG
===========================

1.1.0 (Clip Shrinker 2.9.1)
- Presets work with Fixed bitrate only. With the size limit every clip follows Max size, and the preset list
  in the clip title bars and "Preset (all clips)" are hidden (presets set on clips are kept). They show with
  Fixed bitrate for separate files or a single clip.

1.0.0
- Split out of Clip Shrinker 2.3 into its own plugin: the Bitrate presets window, the preset list in each
  clip's title bar, "Preset (all clips)" in the Export panel and the preset hotkeys.
